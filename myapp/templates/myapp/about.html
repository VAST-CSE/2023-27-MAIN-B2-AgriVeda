About AgriVeda

Empowering Kerala's Farmers through Digital Access

AgriVeda is a comprehensive platform designed to bridge the gap between farmers and the various agricultural schemes provided by the Krishi Bhavans across Kerala. Our mission is to ensure that every farmer has easy, transparent, and timely access to government support.

Our Vision

To digitize and streamline the distribution of agricultural schemes, making it easier for farmers to discover, understand, and apply for financial and technical assistance.

Key Features

  • District-wise categorization of Krishi Bhavan schemes.
  • Secure user authentication and registration.
  • Seamless password recovery through trusted email integration.
  • Up-to-date information on subsidies, mechanization, and insurance.
